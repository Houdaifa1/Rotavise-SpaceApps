# Product goals

One farmer app: compare two future crop cycles across many candidate sequences,
explain alternatives/rejections, and preserve the evidence behind each result.
[Current state](../PROGRESS.md); [architecture and roadmap](ROTAVISE_HANDOFF.md).

| Goal | Deliverable                                                     | Milestone                           |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| G01  | Crop-health history and supported rotation effects              | 04–09                               |
| G02  | Water-demand comparison with honest uncertainty                 | 04–09                               |
| G03  | Historical climate exposure, variability and period comparisons | 06–09                               |
| G04  | Validated photo disease/pest assessment                         | 11                                  |
| G05  | Greenhouse observations and traceable alerts                    | 12                                  |
| G06  | Optional accounts, Google sign-in, saved fields and history     | Backend 07; Google/UI 08; review 09 |
| G07  | Selectable historical weather measurements, dates and charts    | Backend expansion in 06; UI 08      |

## G01 — Crop-health history

Capture crop, dates, observation source and unknown/unconfirmed state. A farmer
report is not a diagnosis. Use reviewed host/break rules and explain their effect;
unsupported history cannot create disease probabilities or treatment advice.
Verify local cases with a specialist and version disagreements/corrections.

## G02 — Water comparison

Separate rainfall, crop demand, reported supply and irrigation adequacy. Compare
compatible evidence; missing demand cannot improve rank. Return “irrigation
adequacy unknown” until a defensible water balance, inputs, uncertainty and reviewed
cases exist. No invented water-saving percentages or measured soil improvements.

## G03 — Climate comparison

Use matching crop/stage windows, complete consecutive analogs, per-variable
coverage and source versions. Sourced thresholds must match the measurement/time
basis. Period changes are descriptive, not forecasts or proof of every hazard.
Validate arithmetic, missing coverage and ranking sensitivity. Add scoring
variables/providers only when supported methods improve a defined decision.

## G04 — Photo assessment

Define crops/issues/image conditions, commercial model/data rights and independent
local field evaluation. Test healthy/look-alike/unsupported/poor images; establish
error and abstention gates. Retain model/evidence version and uncertainty. Results
are not confirmed history or hard exclusions without reviewed applicability.
Require owned bounded uploads, retention/deletion and measured cost/latency.

## G05 — Greenhouse monitoring

Select and verify real hardware/transport, device access, calibration, units,
timestamps and freshness. Show stale/missing/invalid/disconnected states; test
reconnects and delayed/duplicate/out-of-order delivery. Version reviewed alerts.
Keep sensors distinct from outdoor NASA estimates and open-field rules. Validate
real hardware/recovery/privacy/retention. Automatic equipment control is outside scope.

## G06 — Optional accounts and history

Keep temporary private guest planning. Require accounts for persistent fields,
dated crop history and versioned plan inputs/results across visits/devices.
Changing inputs creates a new comparison; saving does not validate a plan.

Step 07 selected maintained Better Auth and implemented verified email/password.
Step 08 must add optional Google sign-in beside email/password, with Google OAuth
credentials, authorized redirects and a farmer-facing button. Review and test
linking to existing accounts with verified email so saved data never moves to a
different owner or becomes accessible through an unverified identity. Require
sign-in/out, recovery, expiry/revocation and account deletion.
Enforce and test ownership on every read/write/list/job, including cross-account
and guest/account isolation in real PostgreSQL.

Claims require explicit confirmation, an authenticated account and proof of the
live guest session; transfer atomically and repeatably. No email/location/ID matching;
expired/deleted data stays unrecoverable. Define account retention separately from
one-day guest requests, including active jobs, evidence and backups on deletion.
Test recovery/verification, fixation/revocation, rate limits, credential expiry,
CSRF where relevant and secure browser sessions. Keep secrets out of logs/URLs.

## G07 — Weather explorer

Select location, date range and any supported measurements: minimum/maximum/mean
temperature, rain, humidity, 2 m wind and solar energy. Show charts/tables, units,
missing dates, coverage, retrieval/source identity and grid limitations. Respect
per-variable date limits. The browser receives no internal token. Additional
variables such as pressure require a defined purpose and validated integration.
Retrieval/display does not automatically add a variable to crop scoring.

Steps 07–10 complete the farmer planner before 11–12. A goal is not authorization
for another milestone, deployment, subscriptions, model integration or hardware purchases.
