## What is wrong today

Data is split across two local PostgreSQL databases in the container `rotavise-dev-postgres-1`:

- `rotavise_dev` (the main one): future-climate units and tiles (past 1985 to 2009, SSP2-4.5 and SSP5-8.5 2026 to 2065), GRACE (regional_water_storage), and only two OLD catalogs (0.1.0 and 0.3.0). It has no current catalog, no GEDI, no fields.
- `rotavise_live` (a test copy from the real NASA runs on the Tadla pilot field, 2026-11-09): the pilot field (32.40 N, 6.55 W, about 0.4 ha), its boundary, its three completed AppEEARS extractions (terrain, ecostress, modis) with raw files, the GEDI Morocco grid (regional_gedi_biomass, about 1.0 million rows), and a duplicate GRACE copy.
  The app must run on ONE database that has everything, so every search reads one place.
