## The problem (found by B6 on 2026-11-12, real data)

For three real points outside Tadla (Souss-Massa 30.4 N 9.6 W, Gharb 34.3 N 6.3 W, Oriental 34.7 N 1.9 W), in suggest mode with all 165 to 169 schedulable crops, the ranked rotations contained only six crops (amaranth, berseem, arugula, chard, cowpea, endive) and the future-climate and near-term blocks all returned `CLIMATE_NOT_COMPARED` although every tile was stored and complete: 'the engine could not form a complete baseline climate comparison for every finalist'. A farmer in those regions would get odd minor-crop suggestions and no future-climate answer. That is not production-grade.
