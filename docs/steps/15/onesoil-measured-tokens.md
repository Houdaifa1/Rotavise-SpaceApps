# OneSoil: measured design values (2026-11-11, from the live yield app at 1440 px)

Measured with `getComputedStyle` over about 190 visible elements of the signed-in app. These are facts about the pattern to match; our own tokens, fonts and icons are used in the build, never theirs.

| What                    | Measured value                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | What it tells us                                                                                            |

| Font                    | **Graphik** (commercial) on nearly every element, `system-ui` on a few                                                                                                                                                                                          | We cannot use it. Pick an open, clean, neutral grotesque of similar proportions (not Inter): decided in F0. |

## What to match and what to avoid

Match: 14 px base, two weights, near-black text with two greys, white panels on light grey, dark rounded chips floating on the map, 8 px control radius, soft shadow only on floating chrome, compact 32 px controls, one green for the action.
Avoid (flaws seen, see onesoil-inspection.md): translucent menus over the map, unexplained jargon, stale toasts, leaked placeholders, empty tabs.
Our identity on top: the crop strip as the logo and core visual, colours with meaning only on data (water, heat, vegetation, bare, caution), the evidence chips, four-language crop names.
