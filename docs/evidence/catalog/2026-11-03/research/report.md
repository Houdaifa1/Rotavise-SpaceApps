**Conflicts and interpretation limits**

- **Tadla sugar-beet yield:** the same page reports 70 and 65 tonnes per hectare for the same 2019-2020 campaign. The exact original quotations are in JSON. Both records remain low-confidence alternatives; neither is selected or averaged. [S23](https://ormvatadla.ma/betterave-a-sucre/).
- **RothC moisture sign convention:** page 11 defines a negative maximum TSMD; page 14 prints a less-than wet-branch condition, while Figure 3 plots positive deficit magnitudes. The printed equations are transcribed with this ambiguity explicitly open. The follow-up verifies that official v1.0.0 sets the temperature modifier to zero below -5 degrees Celsius. Its moisture branch uses greater-than for signed deficits; its 0.556 bare-soil multiplier differs slightly from division by 1.8 in the guide. These version/rounding details remain explicit. [S31](https://www.rothamsted.ac.uk/media/8/download), pp. 10-11 and 14.
- **Allium return intervals:** ONCA gives a general four-to-five-year range and a three-to-five-year disease-specific range on the same page. Both onion and explicitly included garlic records preserve the ranges; no endpoint is chosen. [S19](https://ardna.org/admincockpit/files/contenus_files/ticket_file621e306b6bc34-N1077-r%C3%A9f%C3%A9rentiel-culture-maraich%C3%A8res_VD-Ardna.pdf), p. 57.
- **Potato/Solanaceae:** PNTTA gives five-year spacing in nematode guidance; ONCA gives a three-year minimum in its Verticillium passage. These are different disease contexts, not interchangeable universal intervals. [S29](https://ardna.org/admincockpit/files/contenus_files/ticket_file64216c8e1830d-btta_98.pdf), pp. 1-2; [S19](https://ardna.org/admincockpit/files/contenus_files/ticket_file621e306b6bc34-N1077-r%C3%A9f%C3%A9rentiel-culture-maraich%C3%A8res_VD-Ardna.pdf), p. 31.
- **Rapeseed:** the general ONCA prevention passage gives four years, whereas its phoma advice gives at least three years, particularly in reduced-tillage settings. Both remain separate. [S17](https://ardna.org/admincockpit/files/contenus_files/ticket_file621e396c5d44d-N1077_PhIII_Referentiel_Oleagineuses_VD_1-Ardna.pdf), pp. 82 and 90.
- **Sesame publisher units:** ORMVAT prints an annual production range of 3.7-6.6 tonnes alongside its area and yield statements. The scale appears inconsistent; the production unit and campaign labels need publisher clarification. No corrected unit or reconstructed yield is supplied. [S22](https://ormvatadla.ma/sesame/).
- **Faba-bean heat text:** the ONCA meiosis passage says fertility increases above the stated temperature. It cannot be silently rewritten as sterility or used as a damage threshold. The non-local chamber yield-response evidence remains partial. [S16](https://ardna.org/admincockpit/files/contenus_files/ticket_file6197a6d5ca53c-N1077-Referentiel-legumineuse-Ph-III_VD_1.pdf), p. 28; [S04](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5763371/).
- **Tuta historical dating:** the leaflet describes first reporting in `Avril 2008`, while the practical guide describes introduction in `mai 2008`. These are different descriptions and are not forced into one event date. Publication dates are not inferred from download paths or filenames. [S21](https://www.onssa.gov.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Tuta_avril_2010.pdf), PDF p. 1; [S20](https://www.onssa.gov.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/guide_pratique_gestion_de_la_tomate.pdf), PDF p. 2.
- **Carrot search-index discrepancy resolved:** both saved technical-sheet versions show five years. A six-year search-index rendering was rejected after visual inspection of the saved PDF. [S29](https://ardna.org/admincockpit/files/contenus_files/ticket_file64216c8e1830d-btta_98.pdf), p. 4.
- **Contract and host boundaries:** months and stand years were not converted to fixed days. Winter chickpea was not assigned to spring chickpea. Feverole was not substituted for dry-grain faba bean. Crop/genus-specific rotation advice was not expanded to whole families. Symptoms described on tomato were not asserted on its other Tuta hosts. Published misspellings and historical organism names remain flagged for taxonomic review.
