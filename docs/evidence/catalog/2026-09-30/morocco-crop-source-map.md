## Finding

There is no single authoritative, current, field-level database of every crop
grown in Morocco plus its local sowing, maturity, soil, water, disease and
rotation rules. Combine sources according to the question they actually answer:

An additional Tadla crop is worth **researching**, not importing yet: [ORMVAT
reports sesame as a roughly four-month secondary crop after autumn cereals or
sugar beet](https://ormvatadla.ma/sesame/). This directly matters to a two-cycle
planner, but the page does not provide a reviewed cultivar-specific date interval,
water balance, soil constraints or rotation release rule.

[ONCA's farmer-facing ARDNA index](https://www.ardna.org/fr/articles/thematiques?id=1)
is a useful discovery route for more region-specific extension guides; every
linked guide still needs its own date, geography and page review. Moroccan farmer
articles were also checked. A [2016 AgriMaroc faba
sheet](https://www.agrimaroc.ma/fiche-technique-de-la-culture-de-la-feve-au-maroc/)
gives a narrower soil pH preference than the ONCA legume sheet already captured
in the catalog. A [2016 sesame
sheet](https://www.agrimaroc.ma/fiche-technique-de-la-culture-du-sesame-au-maroc/)
describes a general April–May to October–November schedule, unlike ORMVAT's
local four-month post-cereal/post-beet pattern. Different harvest products or
systems may explain this; the articles do not resolve it. Neither article was
promoted to a rule or used to override primary local evidence.

## Source quality and extraction rules

1. Keep **crop presence**, **variety registration**, **agronomic rule**, and
   **field measurement** separate. A statistic cannot be promoted to a rule.
2. Record publication/campaign year and geography. Tadla, the whole
   Béni Mellal–Khénifra region and Morocco are different scopes; irrigated and
   rainfed production cannot share an assumed schedule.
3. Specify harvest product, cultivar, sowing method, stage, unit, observation
   medium, rule severity, and whether a number is a reference or hard limit.
   A green-pod date cannot become dry-grain maturity; annual rainfall cannot
   become irrigation adequacy.
4. Preserve exact document/page/table provenance and a source snapshot/checksum
   before machine extraction. Existing three-crop source hashes are in
   `data/catalog/releases/0.1.0/sources.json`. The newly linked pages and PDFs
   above have **not** been downloaded, hashed and page-reviewed as new rules in
   this survey.
5. Require a local agriculture specialist to review the resulting rules and
   recorded farmer cases. This survey involved no specialist and makes no
   claim that the present catalog is ready to recommend crops.

## Next evidence work

Start with the three existing crops, not dozens of production profiles. For
each, collect current regional extension/processor guidance, selected cultivar
and system, duration observations, soil/rotation restrictions, applicable weather
and water metrics, and conflicting evidence. Review with a Tadla/Béni Mellal
specialist using actual field cases. Add sesame or lentil only when their
complete profile solves an observed farmer use case. A broad searchable crop
directory may include more names earlier, but it must mark crops without
reviewed profiles as **unsupported for planning**.
