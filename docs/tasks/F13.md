# F13 — "Not cropland" warning when a field is drawn

**Needs:** B05 · **Board:** [BOARD.md](BOARD.md) · **How:** [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md)

When a drawn or suggested field is mostly not cropland according to B05, show
a calm warning in the field creation step and on the field page ("This area
looks like … on the 2021 ESA WorldCover map; you can keep it"). Never block
saving. Source line and year shown.

**May change:** `apps/web/src/features/fields/**` (creation step, field
detail status), tests, new message keys. **Checks:** `pnpm lint`,
`pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test:web`, field e2e specs. **Done when:** the warning
appears for a field drawn on a town and not for a farm field.
