# F03P — Smooth field drawing and selection on phone and desktop

**Needs:** B03 · **Board:** [BOARD.md](BOARD.md) · **How:** [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md)

## Why

Drawing and choosing a field is the first thing a farmer or judge touches. It
must feel as smooth as a native map app on a phone and with a mouse.

## Do

1. Test the current flow (S04 add a field, field suggestions, editing an
   outline) on the sharp map at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, with touch and
   mouse.
2. Fix what is not smooth: finger dragging of points, adding and undoing
   points, closing the shape, zooming and panning while drawing, snapping,
   clear area feedback, no accidental page scroll, large enough touch targets.
3. Keep every existing behaviour and test of S04.

## May change

`apps/web/src/features/map/**`, `apps/web/src/features/fields/**` (creation
and outline editing), their tests.

## Checks

`pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test:web`, `pnpm test:map`,
`pnpm test:e2e field-creation.spec.mjs` (and other field specs).

## Done when

A field can be drawn, edited and saved quickly on a phone-sized screen and on
desktop, and Houdaifa agrees after trying it.
